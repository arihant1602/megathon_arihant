================================================================================
WHAT SETS US APART
AI Incident Report Assistant — Qualcomm On-Device AI Challenge
================================================================================

MOST TEAMS WILL BUILD: a voice-to-text box that fills one generic form and
calls a cloud LLM to do it.

WE BUILT: a fully on-device reasoning pipeline that understands *which*
incident it's hearing before it decides what to ask next — and never once
leaves the chip to do it.

1. A TWO-TIER NEURAL ARCHITECTURE, NOT ONE MODEL DOING EVERYTHING
   A lightweight neural network runs continuously, on-device, classifying
   intent and extracting fields in real time — the heavier semantic model
   is only woken up when the fast path genuinely can't resolve something.
   This isn't a demo shortcut; it's the actual efficiency argument the
   on-device track is scoring for: cheap inference by default, expensive
   inference only on demand.

2. REAL FUNCTION CALLING, NOT A SCRIPTED DEMO
   The model itself decides when to query zone history, resolve a room
   name, or classify severity — against a live local database, fully
   offline. Every call is logged. Nothing is faked for the stage.

3. DEPARTMENT-AWARE, INCIDENT-SPECIFIC FORMS
   Not one generic slot set — the system identifies the incident type and
   loads THAT type's own form from local config, routes it to the right
   department, and never asks a question the conversation already
   answered. One answer fills every field it touches, automatically.

4. BILINGUAL BY DESIGN
   English, Hindi, and natural code-mixed speech — all understood
   on-device, normalized to a single clean English record every admin can
   read.

5. A SYSTEM THAT KNOWS WHAT IT DOESN'T KNOW
   Every field carries its own evidence, its own confidence, and an
   explicit state — known, unknown, or ambiguous. Nothing is ever
   silently invented. A correction doesn't just overwrite data — it
   invalidates every downstream prediction that depended on it, so the
   final report is always internally consistent.

6. A LIVE, SPATIAL VIEW OF THE BUILDING — NOT A TICKET LIST
   A unique admin portal layout: an indoor floor-plan map where every
   incident highlights its real zone and every reporter appears as a
   location pin. One click on a pin opens the full structured dossier
   instantly. This turns a wall of tickets into a living map of the
   building's actual state.

7. RELATIONSHIP AND TREND INTELLIGENCE, NOT JUST A LOG
   Beyond single-incident capture, a graph-based reasoning layer flags
   when separate reports are likely the same underlying problem, and a
   DynoTears-style causal discovery process mines the incident history in
   the background to surface which recurring factors tend to precede
   which failures — intelligence most teams' systems won't even attempt,
   running as a bounded background job that never slows the live
   conversation down.

8. ZERO CLOUD, PROVEN LIVE
   Every piece above — speech, understanding, extraction, reasoning,
   the map, the dossier — runs on the Snapdragon kit itself. We can
   disconnect the network mid-demo and nothing changes. That's not a
   claim; it's something you can watch us do.

THE BOTTOM LINE: everyone else is shipping a transcription box. We're
shipping a reasoning system that understands incidents, not just words.
